# EMS and this repo: re-review, 24 Nov 2026

**Status: draft for the App Owner.** Written after a read-only look at EMS (the app this repo calls ERP Master) and
at every branch of this repo. Facts and counts: `STATE-2026-11-24.md`. Proposed rules: `PARALLEL-BUILD.md`.

## Short answers

1. **Is this repo complete enough?**
   - *For the work it covers* (Phase 1, the six bundles, Orders and the Sales dashboard, up to 23 Sep): yes. It is a
     thorough build record: requirements, decisions with their reasons, test results, review pages, platform traps,
     re-runnable scripts and a ledger.
   - *For EMS as it is now, and for several builders at once*: no. It stops on 23 Sep. It documents 18 of the 83
     worksheets and 60 of the 298 workflows. It has no map of who owns what and no rule for two builders. Its
     reference tenant is gone, and its scripts refuse to run on the renamed app.
2. **Can EMS be re-reviewed and overhauled?** Yes, as a sequence rather than one rewrite:
   1. agree the rules (`PARALLEL-BUILD.md`) and the owners (`MODULES.md`);
   2. a read-only review, module by module;
   3. a verdict per module (keep, fix or rebuild), agreed with its owner;
   4. the changes, one module at a time under a claim, each ending with its tests and a register scan.

   The review comes first because EMS holds other people's live work, and MyInvois, payments and Point of Sale
   connect to outside systems.
3. **Can we simply continue with this repo and its plan?** Not as they are; the problems are listed below. None of
   them means starting over. Most are solved by adding to the repo, not replacing it.

## Keep

- One app, delivered by duplicate-and-prune; menu groups follow Odoo's apps.
- Odoo field names as aliases and Odoo's English labels.
- The per-worksheet loop: requirements → scripted build, safe to re-run → read-back → UI test → hand-off and review
  page.
- `DECISIONS.md` with dates, reasons and who decided; "record and ignore" for bundles; no deletion without
  approval; `TEST …` records.
- `CLAUDE.md`'s rules on saving controls (append, version-pinned saves) and on reading records.
- `BUILDING.md`'s traps; most still hold. Add the new ones from `TRAPS-HAP-CLI-0.9.md`.

## The re-review, step by step (read-only, except the TEST records in step 3)

For each module, in dependency order: Contacts → Products → Invoicing → Sales → CRM → Activities → Inventory →
Point of Sale.

1. **Register scan:** every object of the module attributed; the unattributed ones listed for its owner.
2. **Conformance** against the module's reference: fields, views, rules, buttons and workflows, then Odoo's
   behaviour rule by rule. `worksheets/14-crm-conformance.md` is the model.
3. **Tests:** run the module's existing test list (the worksheet documents, §3) on `TEST …` records, in the UI and
   by readback. This needs the owner's go-ahead because it writes test records.
4. **Verdict:** keep, a fix list, or rebuild, each with an estimate.

Output per module: a review document, a review page and an updated row in `MODULES.md`.

**Effort, as a guide:** in the Odoo Replica (AI) app, the CRM's first phase (9 worksheets) was planned, built and
tested in about one working day by one builder. Reading and testing a module of EMS should take half a day to a day,
so about a week for the whole app.

## After the review: fix in place or rebuild

| | Good | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| **Fix in place**, module by module | Keeps the data, the ids and the links between modules, and other people's work | Keeps the naming and ownership gaps until they are cleaned up; every change needs a claim and the owner |
| **Rebuild a module** (in EMS, or take it from the Odoo Replica (AI) app once it is accepted there) and retire the old one | Clean ownership, scripted, tested | Duplicate effort; data to migrate; relations and roles to re-point; two versions during the switch |

A sensible default: fix in place where the module's structure already follows Odoo (Phase 1, Sales); decide CRM
after its review (the September conformance check found 19 divergences, and the Odoo Replica CRM shows what a
rebuild gives); keep Inventory and Point of Sale in place unless the review finds structural problems. Each module's
owner decides.
