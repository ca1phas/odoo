Invoice Lines account.move.line

A journal item is one line of an invoice. The lines a person edits are the product, section and note lines — Odoo writes the tax and payment-term lines itself, and those belong with the bundles. This worksheet is mounted inside 06's Invoice Lines tab, and it is what makes 06's four amounts real: Untaxed Amount stops being a seeded figure and becomes the sum of its lines.

Worth knowing before you review

The sum of the lines is the point

Open INV/2026/00001 and its three lines add to 11,432.00, which plus the seeded tax of 1,143.20 is the 12,575.20 the tenant shows. Two workflows keep that true: one when a line is added or changed, one when a line is removed. Tax is deliberately not computed — that needs the Taxes bundle, which the owner's direction of 16 September keeps out, so a line has a quantity, a price, a discount and a subtotal, and stops there. One trap to know before you test a deletion: hap worksheet record delete silently suppresses workflows unless --trigger-workflow is passed, so a line removed from the command line without it leaves the invoice stale. Removing one from the browser is the single check this hand-off could not drive from automation, and it is worth your one click.

The subtable, and what it does to 06

The worksheet is mounted into the Invoices tab Invoice Lines, under the remark block 06 left there, so an invoice shows its lines where Odoo shows them — and it stays an ordinary worksheet with its own list, which is Odoo's standalone Journal Items. Two workflows keep the parent in step; none of 06's controls was replaced.

Differences from Odoo

  1. 1
    No drag handleHAP behaviour

    Odoo reorders lines by dragging; a HAP subtable has none, so Sequence is an editable number and the first column — the same decision as Journals' Sequence.

  2. 2
    No Taxes column, and no tax-inclusive AmountBundle

    A line has a subtotal and no tax, and the invoice's Tax stays the tenant's seeded figure. Nothing here stands in for either.

  3. 3
    No Account columnBundle

    Nothing double-entry — the Chart of Accounts. Odoo's “Missing required account on accountable line” cannot be built without it.

  4. 4
    Odoo nulls a section's figures; here a rule hides themNote

    A value typed before the Display Type changed stays on the record, which is why the roll-up filters on Display Type as well as hiding.

  5. 5
    Every unit is offeredHAP behaviour

    Odoo restricts Unit to the product's own unit and packagings; that domain needs a lookup of a relation, which HAP stores as a title.

  6. 6
    Picking a product fills nothing inNote

    Odoo's onchange writes the Label, the Unit and the Price; here all three are typed or seeded.

  7. 7
    Two lines point at a variant the tenant does not useDecision

    The archived original variants are not created; those lines point at each product's single variant and keep the tenant's Label.

  8. 8
    The roll-up follows a change, it does not guarantee itHAP behaviour

    Moving a line to another invoice leaves the old one stale, and two people saving lines of the same invoice in the same second can both read the same sum. Odoo recomputes inside the write.

  9. 9
    A line is added with + Record, not with three buttonsNote

    Odoo's Add a line, Add a section and Add a note are one row plus the Display Type dropdown here; the product Catalog is the Sales app.

  10. 10
    The other 25 tenant journal items are not hereNote

    They belong to documents 06 did not seed, or are the tax and payment-term lines Odoo writes itself.

  11. 11
    A posted document's lines are still editableResolved

    Settled at the end of Phase 1: the subtable joined 06's read-only rule, and a posted document's table now has no Add a row and no Batch Operation — seen on INV/2026/00001, against the Sunway draft where both are there. Odoo locks a posted move's lines the same way. One caveat stands: an interaction rule is browser-side, so the API still writes a posted document's lines, which is why the two roll-up workflows are unaffected.

  12. 12
    A tooling trap, not a differenceRead before testing

    The delete roll-up does fire — 570.00 → 450.00 with the run recorded. But hap worksheet record delete sends triggerWorkflow: false unless --trigger-workflow is passed, so a deletion made from the command line without it removes the line and leaves the invoice stale.
