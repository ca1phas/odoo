Main processes, run in both apps

The sale from quotation to payment, run by hand through each app's own screens on test orders for the customer Casimir. Odoo is the reference. Where Nocoly fell short, it was fixed the same day and run again.

01

Start a quotation for a customer

Fixed 14 Sep
Odoo

Expiration shows today + 30 days (Oct 14). Picking the customer fills in the addresses, the pricelist (Default) and the salesperson; the team is Sales.

Nocoly

Addresses fill in straight away. Pricelist, salesperson and team fill in a few seconds after Submit.

Fixed: Expiration was blank. It now opens as today + 30 days (checked on a new form: 2026-10-14). New orders were also getting the team Online, because all five teams had a blank Sequence. They now carry Odoo's values (Sales 0, the others 10), so the Sales team comes first. The team fix hasn't yet been re-run on a new quotation.

02

Add product lines and pick a variant

Open
Odoo

Choosing the Ergonomic Office Chair opens Configure your product. Pick Blue and the line reads "Ergonomic Office Chair (Blue)" at RM 959 (899 + 60 extra).

Nocoly

The line links to the product only, with no colour choice, so the chair stays at RM 899. Unit and price fill in at once; description and tax fill in after save.

In progress: Order Lines now has a Variant field, limited to the chosen product's variants, and a stored Variant Extra Price. Still to do: show Variant in the order's lines table, add the extra price when a line is priced (list price + extra; fixed-price rules unchanged, as in Odoo), and put the variant name in the description. Until then, run 1 totals RM 2,895.80 in Nocoly against RM 3,027.80 in Odoo, and the whole difference is the RM 120 extra.

Set up

Pricelists and price rules

Odoo
Sales›Products›Pricelists

Rules by product, category or all products, with minimum quantity and dates; fixed price, % discount or formula. Prices update as you add a line or change its quantity.

Nocoly
Products›Pricelists

Same rules, applied in Odoo's order: fixed price into Unit Price, percentage as Disc.%.

Prices a new line a few seconds after save, not as you type. Changing an existing line's quantity doesn't reprice it until Update Prices. Only fixed and percentage rules are built, and a variant's extra price isn't added. Existing lines keep the prices they were created with in both apps.

Set up

Quotation templates and headers/footers

Odoo
Sales›Configuration›Sales Orders›Templates · Headers/Footers

Picking a template fills the quotation's lines, expiration, terms and signature/payment settings; headers and footers are added as PDF pages.

Nocoly
Configuration›Templates · Headers/Footers

Implemented: Save as template creates a template from an order; Duplicate uses a template's validity days.

Not implemented: picking a template on a quotation does nothing; headers/footers aren't added to the PDF; a template's confirmation email and signature/payment settings aren't applied.

Neither app has any templates or headers/footers right now — Nocoly's sample ones were removed on 14 September to match Odoo.
