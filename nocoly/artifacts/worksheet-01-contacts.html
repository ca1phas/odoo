Contacts will show “Company, Person” like Odoo

Odoo names a company’s contact “Company, Person” and a nameless address “Company, Delivery” — in lists, cards and every partner picker. Nocoly currently shows the Name alone, so a nameless address is blank in the list and “Unnamed” on its card. The owner chose to match Odoo now: a Display Name formula becomes the title field, then the affected tests are rerun and this page is updated.

History

Built first from the Odoo 19.0 source, then moved to 19.4 the same day, keeping every id: the Person / Company switch removed, DUNS added, the rules and views that depended on the switch retired, Company shown on every contact. A last pass copied Company ID and DUNS on linking, kept archived companies out of the Company picker, and stopped the push automation running for contacts that have nobody under them.

Cleanup

  • The 3 rules the 19.0 build left disabled — Company is hidden on companies, Job Position only for persons, Company ID only on stand-alone companies — were deleted in the form designer with the owner’s approval.
  • The workflows of a duplicate Archive / Unarchive pair were already gone: HAP deletes a button’s workflow with the button.

Differences from Odoo seen in testing

  1. 1
    Display nameBeing fixed

    Odoo shows “Company, Person” and “Company, Delivery”; Nocoly shows the Name alone. The owner chose to match Odoo — see the top of the page.

  2. 2
    Archive and UnarchiveNote

    The button that does not apply is greyed out rather than hidden.

  3. 3
    “Modifying form data” barHAP behaviour

    After adding a contact from the Contacts tab, or picking a Salesperson, an open record keeps a Modifying form data — Cancel / Save bar although the change is already stored. Clicking Save clears it.

  4. 4
    Kanban cardNote

    The image sits on top of the card; Odoo puts the avatar on the left.
