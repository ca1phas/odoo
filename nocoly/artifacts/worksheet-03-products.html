Worth knowing before you review

Most of Odoo’s product form is left out on purpose

Taxes, Category, Tags, Invoicing Policy, Track Inventory, Barcode, the Attributes & Variants and Prices tabs and the Malaysian codes each arrive with the app or bundle that owns them — check Not built now before reporting a missing field. To see a product’s stored values, run ~/.hap-venv/bin/python nocoly/build/products.py product "Name"; products.py verify compares all 14 products with Odoo.

Differences from Odoo seen in testing

  1. 1
    Unit and Packagings pickers list names onlyNote

    Odoo’s dropdown hints each unit’s ratio (“Days 8.0 Hours”); HAP shows the name. Its search still reads Contains and Reference Unit: “Hours” finds Minutes and Days. As on Units & Packagings.

  2. 2
    Internal Notes has no placeholderHAP behaviour

    HAP’s rich-text editor never shows a field’s hint, even when clicked into. Odoo shows “This note is only for internal purposes.” in the empty field.

  3. 3
    Favorite is a checkbox, not a starNote

    In an open record the change is kept with Save on the Modifying form data bar, and the open view re-sorts only after Refresh.

  4. 4
    Checkbox quick filters have two states once usedHAP behaviour

    Sales, Purchase and Favorite start unset; a click filters for ticked, the next for unticked — the box then looks empty but still filters. Reload the view to clear them.

  5. 5
    Archive and UnarchiveNote

    The button that does not apply is greyed out rather than hidden, and the record closes once it leaves the open view; Odoo keeps the form open with an Archived ribbon.
