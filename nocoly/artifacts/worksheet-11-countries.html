Worth knowing before you review

This is the one worksheet an Accounting Administrator does not own

Odoo's own access list reads countries to everyone and writes them from base.group_system alone — the settings administrator, which none of the four accounting groups stands for. Odoo's Countries list even ships with New and Delete switched off. So all four business roles have view here, and only an app Administrator creates, edits or deletes a country. The owner chose this deliberately over the app's usual pattern. Note that res.country.state is not the same — the States bundle will not inherit it.

No Active field, so no Archive and no Unarchive — res.country has none, as Product Categories has none. No reverse field either: Odoo's country form does not list the partners in that country, so the relation is one-way. What Odoo's form does show at the foot is the country's States, and that is the next bundle.

What it changed on Contacts

  • Country became a relation (country_id), in the text control's place beside ZIP. The stand-in held the alias country while Odoo's field is country_id, so the relation claimed the real name from the start.
  • Eight contacts carried — every one whose Country text read Malaysia — read back, and then the text control was deleted.
  • The Country column, the Country quick filter, the Kanban card's country and both address automations were re-pointed at the relation before the deletion, not after: a deleted control stays named in every view and workflow step that used it.
  • State stays Text. Odoo's rule that changing a country clears a state belonging elsewhere needs states, so it belongs to the next bundle.

Not built now

Named on the form itself, in a remark block at its foot: the Currency (res.currency is not among the six bundles), the flag (a computed URL to an image on the Odoo server), the three Advanced Address Formatting fields that Odoo shows only in developer mode, the Country Groups, and the States. Also left out: the code forced upper-case on save (a HAP rule compares values, it cannot transform one), the two-letter search that jumps to the code, and the VAT label renaming a contact's Tax ID field.

Built by nocoly/build/countries.py. The 251 records were rebuilt from this repo's own Odoo base data and verified against the tenant by digest rather than copied through the browser — count, an FNV-1a hash, the sum of the calling codes and the state and ZIP counts all matched; only three VAT labels differed (Hong Kong, the United States, Indonesia) and the tenant's values won. Every one was then read back field by field: 0 missing, 0 differing.

Two things the build found

  • The API does not enforce what the form does. No duplicates is refused on API writes on both fields — but the two-character limit is form-side only, and Zip Required's default is not applied through the API. The seed checks the length itself.
  • Section 1 missed two automations. Four workflow nodes named the Country text control: both of Contacts' address syncs, a branch, and a trigger's field list. Odoo syncs country_id with the other five address fields, so they were re-pointed at the relation rather than losing Country — and test 19 and 20 below prove both still work.

Tests and differences

Twenty-three tests in the browser

Run on 18 September 2026 with Odoo open beside the app. Twenty-one pass. One failed and was fixed during the test: the calling code drew as 1,264 because HAP's thousandth setting reads the opposite way round from its name — "0", the platform default, draws the separator and "1" hides it. Anguilla now reads 1264, as Odoo does. The last test was run later the same day, once the owner switched on HAP's role debugging: seen as Invoicing — and as Accounting Administrator — the Countries view has no + Record, a country's form is read-only, and the Country picker on a contact offers no + Record either. That is how Odoo's no_create is enforced here.

What the tests cover: the view and its two columns; Malaysia, the United States and Angola field by field against Odoo's own screens; both uniqueness refusals, the length limit and the two required fields; Zip Required's default on a new record; search by name and by code; and on Contacts — the relation in the text field's place, the column, the quick filter, the Kanban card, the Archived view, and both address automations end to end: a new contact under a company inherits the company's address and its Country, and changing the company's Country to Singapore carried to its four people and back to Malaysia again, while the Invoice and Delivery addresses stayed put, exactly as Odoo's _children_sync behaves.

Differences from Odoo

  1. 1
    Åland Islands sorts second here and last in OdooSorting

    Ours puts Å beside A; the tenant's database collation puts it after Z, so Odoo's list ends Zambia · Zimbabwe · Åland Islands. The only row of 251 where the two orders disagree.

  2. 2
    The picker offers + RecordHAP behaviour

    Odoo's country field is no_create: a contact cannot invent a country. HAP has no such switch, so what stops it is Roles — and the role check proved it: seen as Invoicing, the picker has no + Record at all.

  3. 3
    A two-letter search does not jump to the codeSearch

    Odoo matches a 2-character search against the code first. Here MY returns Malaysia, Myanmar and Saint Barthélemy, in name order.

  4. 4
    The picker lists the newest record firstHAP behaviour

    As on Payment Terms — with 252 countries that means the tail of the alphabet at the top. Odoo's picker is in name order.

  5. 5
    Clicking a country chip opens the countryHAP behaviour

    Odoo's field carries no_open: True.

  6. 6
    No flag, no currency, no address layout, no statesScope

    All in Not built now, and all named in the remark block at the foot of the form.
