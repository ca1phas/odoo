Nocoly Ground-Up Build

Eleven phases. One builds the worksheets every app relates into; the other ten are Odoo's own app groups, added whole. Within each phase, worksheets and their relations go in one at a time, in dependency order.

11 phases234 worksheets~38 weeks at 40 h/wkca1phas/odoo @ 7bbce82414 Sep 2026

The shape

One foundation, ten app groups

Phase 1 is the only phase that isn't an app. It holds the worksheets that more than one group points a Relation control at. Only seven of those are unavoidable; the rest belong to optional features and are deferred to the phase that first needs them. Nothing here belongs to a single app, which is why it is built first and never moved afterwards.

Everything after it is an Odoo catalogue group taken whole. The Sales group splits into its three apps because they are the spine of the system and each is worth shipping on its own; the remaining seven groups go in as groups.

Where the build actually is — 18 September 2026. This page is the plan; the app is real. Phase 1 is built. Fourteen worksheets stand in the Nocoly app ERP Master, seeded from a live Odoo 19.4 tenant, UI-tested one at a time and out for review — the seven core, and seven more from six bundles the owner pulled forward into Phase 1 rather than deferring. The slider therefore starts at 1, with those six switched on.

The seven core. Contacts 13/13 · Units & Packagings 18 of 19 · Products 15 of 17 · Product Variants 16/16 · Journals 20 of 21 · Invoices 25/25 · Invoice Lines 19 of 20.

The six bundles, in build order. Product Categories — the tenant's seven categories, 17 of 18 — gave Products the Category it never had. Chart of Accounts — 87 accounts, 28/28 — brought every account field in the app with it, on Contacts, Products, Product Categories, Journals and Invoice Lines, hidden by role as Odoo hides them. Payment Terms — ten terms with their lines, 21 of 22 — is the first that replaced a stand-in: an invoice's Payment Terms text became a real link, the values carried over and the text field deleted, and an invoice now takes its term from the customer and works its Due Date out from it. Countries — Odoo's 251, 22 of 23 — replaced the second stand-in the same way, bringing each country's calling code, VAT label and the two switches that say whether an address needs a state or a postcode. States — 2,102 in 74 countries, 20 of 21 — did the same for a contact's State, gave every country the list of its states, and brought Odoo's two rules with it: a state sets its country, and a country clears a state that belongs elsewhere. Taxes — 35 Malaysian SST taxes, 19 of 23 — is the last of the six and the one that made the arithmetic real: a product carries its sales and purchase taxes, a line carries the taxes it was given, and an invoice's Tax stopped being a figure copied from the tenant. It is now the lines' total less their subtotal, landing on the tenant's own 10,460.32, 1,143.20 and 14,400.00 to the cent.

What Phase 1 still owes. Roles are set — four accounting roles mapped one-to-one onto Odoo's own accounting groups, each one's screens checked with HAP's role debugging — but nobody is assigned to any of them; who belongs where is the owner's call. And nothing has been signed off: all thirteen hand-offs are out for review and none has come back, so the TEST records each review page points at stay where they are. Everything on this page after Phase 1 is still plan.

Phase 1

Core Foundation

7 core · 7 bundle worksheets pulled in · 23 deferred

Seven worksheets are genuinely unavoidable. Everything else in the foundation — taxes, the chart of accounts, categories, payment terms, variants, pricelists — is a feature you switch on when you need it. The rule for deciding: a worksheet is core only if Odoo enforces it with required=True from a model that is itself core, or the concept is unrepresentable without it. Every other worksheet is a bundle, and the evidence for each call is in the table below. Verification

How each call was decided

Read out of the Odoo 19 source rather than assumed. Where a field carries required=True, the database will not let the row exist without it — that is the hard evidence. Where it doesn't, the worksheet is a feature, however much you may want it on day one.

WorksheetVerdictEvidence from the source
ContactscoreNo required=True, but unrepresentable: an invoice with no party is not a business document. The most-referenced model in the repo — 57 modules, 58 extensions.
Units & Packagingscoreproduct.template.uom_id required=True — no product exists without a unit
Productscoreproduct.product.product_tmpl_id required=True
Product VariantscoreEvery order line, invoice line and stock move points at the variant, not the template
Journalscoreaccount.move.journal_id required=True — no move of any kind exists without a journal
Invoicescoreaccount.move.line.move_id and account.payment.move_id both required=True
Invoice LinescoreAn invoice with no lines carries no amount
TaxesbundleNothing in the repository declares required=True on account.tax. account.move.line.tax_ids is computed and optional. Structurally a feature — practically you will switch it on immediately.
Chart of AccountsbundleThe only required=True references come from account.fiscal.position.account — itself an optional bundle. account.journal.default_account_id is not required.
Product Categoriesbundleproduct.template.categ_id is not required in Odoo 19 — no required=True, no default — and nothing else points at it
Payment TermsbundleThe only required=True reference is account.payment.term.line.payment_id — its own line table. account.move.invoice_payment_term_id is optional.
Payment Methods · PaymentsbundleNothing requires either. Your current Sales build already models payment as an Amount Paid field on Invoices — real-world proof that this is a feature, not a foundation.
Activity Typesbundlemail.activity.mixin is an AbstractModel that models opt into. The only worksheet pointing at Activity Types is Plan Steps, itself in the same bundle. Nothing in the core touches it.

Structurally optional is not the same as skippable. Taxes carries no required=True anywhere in Odoo, yet no Malaysian invoice is correct without it and every rollup in your existing build hangs off the Taxes lookup. The distinction the table draws is about build order and blast radius, not importance: a bundle can be added later without touching what already exists, a core worksheet cannot.

Two bundles are cheap now and expensive later. Three corrections now — two from the check, one from building. Taxes needs Geography — account.tax.country_id carries required=True, so a Taxes worksheet with no Countries behind it is incomplete; switch Taxes on in the wall above without Geography and it turns orange. Phase 1 is done, and it came in near the estimate. The seven core worksheets and seven bundle worksheets — 14 of the 37 the phase will eventually hold — are built, seeded and UI-tested. The estimate above puts the core at 48 h and all thirty bundle worksheets at 100 h; what was actually built is the core plus the six bundles the core leans on hardest, and the shape of the work matched the model: creating worksheets was nearly free, and the hours went into relations, workflows, roles and reading everything back. The tail the model warns about is visible here too — three of the six bundles spent most of their time not on the new worksheet but on going back to the worksheets that had been waiting for it.

Two phases distort the total. Website at 9 weeks is a quarter of the plan and is not worksheet work — Nocoly is a database, not a site builder, so eCommerce and public pages mean a separate front end talking to HAP over the v3 API. Treat it as its own project. Inventory & Manufacturing at 7 weeks is genuine: 45 worksheets and the 27-control bridge back into Sales.

Drop both and the working ERP — foundation, CRM, Sales, POS, Services, HR, Finance, Marketing — lands in about 21 weeks, roughly five months.

What the rate assumes. The 40 h/week is your input — and it is the rate you have already demonstrated: the existing Sales app took roughly 100 hours across seven days, which is well above forty a week. The productivity inside it is yours; the productivity inside it is the one you demonstrated — several background agents in parallel, Odoo open beside you as the reference, results read back rather than trusted. A person working the HAP form designer alone would be several times slower. The estimate carries no allowance for rebuilding a worksheet you got wrong, which is exactly what the dependency ordering in this plan exists to prevent.

Milestones worth naming

  • Week 1 — core foundation standing. Seven worksheets, no app yet, but every later phase now has something to relate into. Done, along with six of the bundles.
  • Week 5 — CRM live. First complete app: pipeline, views, roles, rules, a won-to-quotation workflow, and a click-through pass behind it.
  • Week 6 — quote to cash. Orders, Order Lines, Invoices, Payments, quotation PDFs. The point the system starts replacing something.
  • Week 16 — the physical business. Inventory, Purchase and Manufacturing in; delivery-driven invoicing real rather than modelled.
  • Week 28 — everything except the public web.

Throughout

Four rules that hold in every phase

01

A worksheet is finished when its relations are wired

Create the worksheet, add its Relation controls, read them back, then move to the next number. Half-wired tables left for a later pass are how a foundation drifts out of dependency order — and HAP will happily let you point a Relation at a worksheet that doesn't exist yet.

02

Relations point down, never sideways

An app worksheet relates into phase 1 freely. It relates into another app's worksheet only through a named bridge pack — a documented set of controls appended to one side, built after both apps stand. Odoo enforces this with separate modules; here it is a review rule.

03

Bridges are their own deliverable

Nocoly has no auto_install, so the trigger is a checklist. One file per pair — state/bridge_sale_stock.json — naming the controls it appends, the workflows it owns and the invariants it must not break. Never smuggled into either app's build.

04

Every phase ends on a seeded, readable-back check

Seed each new worksheet from the matching Odoo tenant and read it back before declaring the phase done. Several HAP writes return success and store nothing; a phase that was never read back is a phase you will re-debug three phases later.

Worksheet counts are persistent models defined by each module at ca1phas/odoo 7bbce824 (19.0 Community), excluding transient wizards, abstract mixins, and ir.*/report.*/theme.* infrastructure. Models that HAP provides natively — users, groups, messages, followers, attachments, model metadata — are excluded from phase 1. Group membership follows odoo.com/page/all-apps; Enterprise-only apps in each group are not counted.
