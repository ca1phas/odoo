Product Variants product.product

A variant is one sellable version of a product, and it is what order and invoice lines point at. Until the Product Variants bundle every product has exactly one variant, created with it, and Products is the master of what both carry.

Worth knowing before you review

Edit on the product — the variant follows

A variant has no + Record button: it is created with its product. Internal Reference, Cost, Weight, Volume and Favorite are copied from the product by workflows and are read-only on the variant; Name, Product Type, Unit, Sales, Purchase and Sales Price are lookups. Barcode, Extra Packagings and Variant Image are the variant's own. Workflows take up to 20 seconds — refresh before judging. variants.py variant "[CONS-0002] Whiteboard Marker Set" prints any variant's stored values.

Differences from Odoo seen in testing

  1. 1
    Unit pickers list names onlyNote

    Extra Packagings shows unit names without Contains and Reference Unit, as on Products and Units & Packagings.

  2. 2
    Unreferenced variants sort firstHAP behaviour

    Odoo lists them after the referenced ones; HAP puts empty values first in an ascending sort and cannot change it.

  3. 3
    The product's fields are read-only on the variantNote

    Odoo edits Name, Sales Price and image through the variant form; here they are lookups or copies — edit the product.

  4. 4
    Workflows take a few secondsHAP behaviour

    A product change reaches its variant within about 20 seconds and shows after a refresh; variants created by the workflow have no owner.

  5. 5
    Archive and UnarchiveNote

    In a record opened as a full page only the applicable button shows; in the pop-up the other is greyed out. Edits are kept with Save on the Modifying form data bar.
