Journals account.journal

A journal is the book an entry is written in: customer invoices in a Sales journal, vendor bills in a Purchase one, payments in Bank, Cash or Credit Card, everything else in Miscellaneous. Type drives the whole form, and Sequence Prefix becomes the prefix of every entry number — which is why Odoo keeps it short and unique.

Worth knowing before you review

Type decides what the form shows

Journal Name, Type, Sequence Prefix and Sequence sit above Odoo's two tabs, Journal Entries and Advanced Settings, and five rules on Type do the rest. A Sales journal shows the credit note sequence and, on Advanced Settings, Payment Communications (required); Purchase shows the credit note sequence; Bank, Cash and Credit Card show the payment sequence, and Bank and Cash lose the Advanced Settings tab, as in Odoo; Miscellaneous shows neither checkbox. Each tab ends with a note naming what Odoo also keeps there and which bundle brings it. Two other things to know: the 5-character limit on Sequence Prefix is a form check — the API stores longer values, while No duplicates holds everywhere — and Sequence is visible here because a HAP table has no drag handle.

Differences from Odoo seen in testing

  1. 1
    Odoo's two tabs, with a note in eachDecision

    The tabs were built even though the deferred settings leave them thin, so each ends with a remark block naming what Odoo also shows there and which bundle or table brings it. Odoo's Incoming and Outgoing Payments tabs are not built at all.

  2. 2
    Order at equal SequenceNote

    Odoo's list then falls back to creation order (Exchange Difference before Cash Basis Taxes); ours follows the model's declared order, Type then Sequence Prefix. The first four rows are identical.

  3. 3
    Sequence is visible and editableHAP behaviour

    Odoo hides it behind the list's drag handle, which HAP has no equivalent for.

  4. 4
    The 5-character limit is a form check onlyHAP behaviour

    The API stores a longer prefix. No duplicates — what entry numbering depends on — is enforced everywhere.

  5. 5
    No computed prefix or nameNote

    Odoo fills an empty prefix or name from the Type; here both are required, and the placeholders show the pattern.

  6. 6
    Archive and the Save barHAP behaviour

    A full-page record shows only the button that applies, and an edit is kept with Save on the Modifying form data bar.
