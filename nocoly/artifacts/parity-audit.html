Odoo–Nocoly Parity Audit

Every page in your four sections, checked function by function: lists, search, buttons, workflows, create, edit and the record form, plus the data behind them. casimir.odoo.com is the reference.

  • List views only. Odoo’s kanban, calendar, pivot, graph, map and activity views are set aside for now; the Reporting pages are charts by nature, so they’re compared as charts.
  • Quotations are deferred to a later pass. The Configuration section isn’t part of this one.
  • To Invoice pages are view tabs on the Orders sheet, as you set up. Services & Material is hidden because it’s switched off in Odoo.
  • Nothing was changed except your read-only switch on 4 Orders fields and one button test on TechBridge (S00012), which was put back exactly.

Needs attention

  • Defect
    One-sided links

    The Combo Choices items list is empty on every combo. Sales Teams ↔ Orders and Customers ↔ Orders were filled by hand, so new orders won’t appear or count. The Customers Child tab shows nothing.

  • Defect
    Invoices from Create Invoice

    Amount Paid, Amount Due and Payment Reference stay blank (seen on Klinik’s INV/2026/0008). Odoo shows the amount still owing.

  • Quick fix
    List set-up

    Column settings aren’t switched on for 6 sheets (Sales Teams, Pricelists, Attributes, Combo Choices, Categories, Units), so they show every field. No list uses Odoo’s sort order.

  • Note
    Order numbers

    Nocoly’s are one lower than Odoo’s (S00009 = S00010). AutoNumber can’t be rewritten, so compare orders by customer.

  • Note
    Settled on 11 Sep

    Klinik matched to Odoo; Lock/Unlock with read-only rules for locked and cancelled orders; Order Lines calibrated; General Public and Casimir aligned; Internal Reference back on Products; S00016 restored to Quotation Sent.
