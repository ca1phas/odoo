Twenty Buttons

Odoo's Orders form carries twenty actions. They live in four different places, which is why counting only the header finds four. Here is every one, what it does, and what it becomes in Nocoly — in the order we build them.

part 1 · 21 Sep

Where the twenty live. Eleven in <header>. Four as <control> elements inside the lines list — they are not <button> tags at all. One wizard below the lines. Eight as ir.actions.server bound to sale.order, reached from the cog menu. And Sign & Accept is on the customer portal, not in the back office.

The rule for the right-hand column: prefer a Nocoly native feature over a built workflow. Seven of the twenty need nothing built at all because the platform already does them — Discount joined them on 22 Sep.

3

Deliver — the one that walks the lines

Done, 22 Sep — pressed in the browser and read back. Discount turned out not to need building. Deliver does: it copies each line's own Quantity, a different value per row, which a batch edit cannot express.

09

Discount

action_open_discount_wizard · below the lines · hidden when Locked or Cancelled

Odoo opens a wizard and applies a percentage across every line. Here: a button that writes Discount on each row.

Not built — tested 22 Sep. The Order Lines subtable's own Batch Operation → Edit → Discount sets the same percentage on every selected line; saving the order recomputes every line and the three totals. That covers Odoo's default mode, On All Order Lines. Global Discount and Fixed Amount add a discount line and need a discount product — deferred until asked for.

nativeBatch Operation
11

Deliver

server action 504 · deliver_sold_quantity · form, list

Sets each line's Delivered = Quantity. qty_delivered is writable, so this is the shortcut for orders that never touch Inventory.

Built as a sub-process: the button collects this order's product lines, and a small child workflow runs once per line, copying that line's own Quantity. Enabled on Sales Orders only. On S00006 it delivered 12 · 25 · 50 · 25, then was restored; greyed on Cancelled and Quotation Sent orders.

verifiedsub-process

Sign & Accept

the customer portal, not the back office

The customer signs on a public page; Odoo writes signature, signed_by, signed_on and confirms the order.

Nocoly Public Sharing gives the page. The catch: a share link is read-only by default, so accepting a signature needs a form that writes back — not sharing alone.

Conditions read from the sale.order form and list arch on casimir.odoo.com; behaviour read from the model's own methods in addons/sale/models/sale_order.py. Server actions read from ir.actions.server bound to the model. Twenty items: 6 standing · 11 to build · 3 out of reach.
