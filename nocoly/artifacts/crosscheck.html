Read this before you open Odoo yourself

Two things about the reference tenant

Odoo does not render in a background browser tab. Its client waits for an animation frame, and a hidden tab is given none, so the page stops after its top bar and control panel and every screenshot is a dark rectangle. Click into the Odoo window before you read a page. Nocoly's own app draws happily while hidden — which is why only Odoo ever looked broken, and why three worksheets ended up carrying a false "the tenant has no screen for this".

The tenant's user cannot see any account field. casimir is an Invoicing administrator — it holds account.group_account_invoice and account.group_account_manager, but not account.group_account_readonly or account.group_account_user, which belong to the full Accounting app. Odoo puts a journal's five accounts, a product's and a category's income and expense accounts, an invoice line's account and a contact's receivable and payable behind exactly those two groups. They exist and hold values; that screen just will not show them to you. Everything bundle 2 hid from our Invoicing role is a field Odoo hides the same way.

What is new

Three differences, none of which moves a value

  1. 1
    A unit's ratio is called Quantity on Odoo's form02 · label

    Only Odoo's list heads it Contains, which is the word we took. The field, its help text and its value are the same. A one-word rename whenever the owner wants Odoo's exact wording.

  2. 2
    Odoo's invoice list heads the untaxed figure Tax Excluded06 · label

    Ours uses Odoo's own field label, Untaxed Amount. Same figure, same column.

  3. 3
    The invoice list carries Journal where Odoo carries Last Reminder06 · view

    Odoo keeps Journal optional and hidden, and it totals Tax Excluded, Total and Amount Due at the foot of the list. Ours shows Journal and foots nothing.

Two earlier notes corrected

02's test 19 said "the tenant does not show a Units & Packagings menu (its units setting is off), so there is no screen to compare". The menu is there, the setting is on, and the screens match unit for unit. The same trap — a hidden browser tab, not a missing page — is why 04 and 06 had only ever been compared against data. All three are compared now.

One thing the Taxes pass caught that the build had not. Odoo's Invoice Lines tab carries a Taxes column between the price and the amount; ours had gained Total without it, so a line showed a tax-inclusive amount with no way to see or change the tax behind it. It was added during that worksheet's UI test.

And one trap worth repeating for Taxes in particular. The Odoo 19.0 source in this repository is out of date for account.tax: 19.4 has moved Description up into the header and Tax Scope and Active down into Advanced Options, and three installed modules extend the form that a source read never shows. The comparison was made against the tenant's own stored view, not the source.
