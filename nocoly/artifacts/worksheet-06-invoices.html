Invoices account.move

One model sits behind seven Odoo menus. A customer invoice, a vendor bill, either kind of credit note, a receipt and a plain journal entry are all the same record — Type decides which, and Odoo's menus are filtered views of the one table. A document starts as a Draft with no number; confirming it posts it and takes a number from its Journal, which is the link back to 05.

Worth knowing before you review

Confirm is where the work happens

There are three buttons, not Odoo's ten. Confirm posts a draft and builds its number the way Odoo's sequence does — the journal's Sequence Prefix, the year of the Accounting Date and five digits, so the Sales journal gives INV/2026/00001 and a credit note off a journal with a Dedicated Credit Note Sequence gives RINV/2026/00001. It also fills an empty Invoice Date with today on anything but a journal entry and, on a customer document, copies the new number into the Payment Reference. Cancel asks first; Reset to Draft takes a document back and keeps its number. Two things to keep in mind while reviewing: a document that already has a number keeps it through Reset to Draft and the next Confirm, as in Odoo, and a new number is taken one past the highest already issued on that journal and year — so gaps are harmless but two people confirming in the same second could still collide, because HAP has no row lock; and account.move has no Active field, so this is the first worksheet with no Archive.

Differences from Odoo

  1. 1
    One field, two wordsNote

    Odoo renames partner_id per type — Customer on a customer document, Vendor on a vendor one — and invoice_date likewise (Invoice Date / Bill Date). HAP cannot rename a field from a rule, so Customer / Vendor carries both words and Invoice Date keeps the customer wording, with a rule making it required on a vendor document.

  2. 2
    Journal and Accounting Date are always shownNote

    Odoo shows the Journal only when several suit the type, and the Accounting Date only on vendor documents or once a posted invoice's date differs. Both are always visible here — the Journal because Confirm numbers from it.

  3. 3
    Two confirmations in the same second could collideHAP behaviour

    The number is read one past the highest already issued, which survives gaps and deletions, but a HAP workflow has no row lock where Odoo's sequence takes one. Number therefore allows duplicates by design — every draft holds the word “Draft”.

  4. 4
    A draft's Number literally reads “Draft”Decision

    Odoo stores / and displays Draft; here the field holds the word, because it is the worksheet's title and a title shows in every picker, card and relation.

  5. 5
    No Active field, no ArchiveNote

    account.move has none — a document is cancelled instead — so this worksheet has no Archived view and no Archive / Unarchive buttons, unlike the five before it.

  6. 6
    Payment Terms and Recipient Bank are text stand-insDecision

    And the four amounts are seeded read-only figures until 07 Invoice Lines turns them into roll-ups of the lines. Each field says so in its own description.

  7. 7
    Odoo's header carries ten buttonsNote

    Plus ribbons, alerts and smart buttons; here there are three buttons and a read-only Status field. The rest are in Not built now.

  8. 8
    A relation picker offers archived recordsHAP behaviour

    The Journal picker lists the archived TEST Sales journal beside Sales; Odoo hides archived journals from the field.

  9. 9
    A button whose condition fails is hidden, not greyed outHAP behaviour

    A draft shows Confirm and Cancel; a posted or cancelled document shows Reset to Draft. Journals behaves the same way — its own hand-off said “greyed out”, and that has been corrected.

  10. 10
    A new record stays in a view that excludes it until you refreshHAP behaviour

    A Vendor Bill created from the Invoices view stayed in that view until the page was reloaded, then dropped out correctly. The view's filter is right; the grid just does not re-apply it to the row you have just made.
