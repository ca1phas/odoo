1

Finding your way

Everything for selling sits under Sales in the left sidebar.

  • Orders — quotations and sales orders. This is where you spend your day.
  • Order Lines — every line of every order, when you want to search across orders rather than open one.

Customers live under Contacts, what you sell under Products, and invoices under Invoicing. A quotation and a sales order are the same record at different stages, so nothing moves between lists as it progresses — only its status changes.

2

Writing a quotation

Open Orders and press + Record.

  1. Customer is the only thing you must fill in. Picking one fills the invoice address, the delivery address and the payment terms from that customer's own record.
  2. Expiration is set a month ahead. It is the date the quotation stops being signable.
  3. Add your lines (next section), then Submit.

The number — S00001, S00002 and so on — is given when you save, and never changes afterwards.

Two dates, one field. Quotation/Order Date is the day you wrote the quotation, and it is rewritten to the moment you press Confirm. That is deliberate: a confirmed order is dated when it became an order.
3

Lines, taxes and totals

The Order Lines tab holds what you are selling.

Press Add a row. Each row has a Display Type, which decides what the row is:

Display typeWhat it is
ProductSomething you are charging for. Picking the product fills the description, price, unit and taxes.
SectionA heading that groups the rows under it. Carries no figures.
SubsectionA heading inside a section.
NoteA line of explanation for the customer. Carries no figures.

Quantity starts at 1 and Discount at 0. Subtotal, Tax Amount and Total work themselves out, and the three figures at the foot of the tab — Untaxed Amount, Tax, Total — add up the lines.

Tax Mode, above the lines, says whether your prices exclude or include tax. It is fixed once the quotation is confirmed.

Changing many lines at once. Tick the rows, press Batch Operation → Edit, and set a field on all of them — a discount on every line, say. The change only lands when you save the order.
4

Discounts

Two ways, depending on whether the discount belongs to a line or to the whole order.

A discount on one line

Type a percentage into that line's Discount. The line's subtotal follows immediately.

A discount on the whole order

  1. Set Discount Type — Global Discount for a percentage off, Fixed Amount for an amount in RM off the total including tax.
  2. Put the number in Discount Value: 10 for 10%, or 500 for RM 500.
  3. Press Apply Discount.

A discount line appears at the foot of the order — one per tax rate, so the tax comes off correctly. Change the value and press again, and the lines are rewritten. Set the value to 0 and press, and they go.

Apply Discount owns those lines. Every discount line on the order is replaced each time you press it, including one you added by hand.
5

Printing it

The printer icon at the top right of a record.

You are offered the layout that suits the record:

LayoutWhen it appears
QuotationWhile the order is a quotation or has been sent
Sales OrderOnce it is confirmed
Quotation / OrderAlways — the same page with the status in its heading

Each prints your company heading, the customer and both addresses, the dates, the lines with their taxes, the three totals, the terms and the payment terms. You can print it or download it as a Word file.

Terms and conditions sits under the lines. Whatever you type there prints at the foot of the page. It starts empty on every order.
6

Sending it

Send Quotation emails the customer.

It attaches the printed quotation, and moves the order to Quotation Sent. On a confirmed order it sends an order confirmation instead. If the customer has no email address, nothing is sent and you get a notice instead.

Sent it yourself, by your own email or by hand? Press Mark as Sent, which moves the status without sending anything.

Sending needs credits on the account. Until they are topped up, Send Quotation stops partway and no email leaves. Print the quotation and send it yourself, then press Mark as Sent.
7

Letting the customer sign

A link they open, sign, and that confirms the order.

  1. On the quotation, tick Online Signature (Other Info → Confirmation) if it is not ticked already.
  2. Press Share for Signature. A link appears in Signing Link, under the signature fields.
  3. Copy it to the customer however you like — email, WhatsApp, a message.

The customer opens it without logging in and sees the quotation: customer, dates, lines, totals and terms, all read-only. They draw a signature, type their name and press Accept & Sign.

The order then records the signature, the name and the moment, and confirms itself — status Sales Order, dated now. You get a notice in the app.

DetailBehaviour
Used twiceThe link works once. Afterwards it only says "Submitted".
Expired quotationThe link stops working on the Expiration date, and the button is not offered.
Online Payment tickedThe order keeps the signature but stays a quotation, waiting for payment.
Passed on to someone elseAnyone holding the link can sign. Send it to the person who should.
8

Confirming, cancelling, reopening

The buttons across the top of a record change with its status.

ButtonWhat it doesWhen you can press it
ConfirmTurns the quotation into a sales order and dates it nowA quotation, sent or not, with a product on every line
CancelCancels the orderAnything not locked
Set to QuotationPuts it back to a plain quotation and clears the signatureA cancelled or already-sent order
Mark as SentMarks it sent without emailingA quotation

A greyed button means that action does not apply to this record — hover it and the app says why.

Confirm refuses a line with no product. Every line that is not a section, subsection or note needs one. The message names the problem, but the screen also shows a green "Operation completed" — trust the message, not the green.

Locked

Tick Locked on an order you want left alone. A locked order cannot be cancelled until you untick it. It can still be delivered and invoiced.

9

Delivering

Deliver on a sales order.

It sets every product line's Quantity Delivered to its Quantity — the shortcut for an order that went out in full. For a part delivery, type the figures into the lines yourself instead.

10

Invoicing

Create Invoice makes the invoice from the order.

It makes a draft invoice for everything not yet invoiced, carrying the customer, the addresses, the terms and the lines. The order's Invoices list and the invoice's Sales Orders list then point at each other.

  1. Press Create Invoice — on one order, or over several you have ticked.
  2. Open the draft, check it, and press Confirm to post it. That is when it gets its number.
  3. Invoice something later — a second delivery, say — by pressing Create Invoice again. Only what is left is invoiced.

Invoice Status on the order keeps itself up to date:

StatusMeaning
Nothing to InvoiceNothing outstanding — often because nothing has been delivered yet
To InvoiceThere is something to bill; the order shows in the To Invoice list
Fully InvoicedEverything on the order has been billed
Upselling OpportunityMore was delivered than ordered — worth a call before you bill
Done with an order that will never be fully billed? Tick Invoicing Closed and it stops appearing in To Invoice. Untick it to bring it back.
11

Templates and repeat orders

For the quotation you write over and over.

  1. Write it once with its lines, tick Is Template and give it a Template Name. It leaves the quotation lists and appears under Templates.
  2. To use it, open it, then choose Recreate from the record menu (···).
  3. You get a fresh quotation with the lines already in it. Untick Is Template on the copy, set the customer, and carry on.
Recreate, not Copy. Copy duplicates the heading and leaves the lines behind. Recreate brings the lines.

The lists you work from

The tabs across the top of Orders.

ListWhat it holds
QuotationsEverything not yet confirmed — your pipeline
OrdersConfirmed sales orders
To InvoiceSales orders with something left to bill
To UpsellSales orders where more went out than was ordered
TemplatesYour saved starting points
AllEverything, templates included

Any list can be narrowed further with the filter icon, and sorted by clicking a column. The Orders list also has an Invoice Status filter at the top for a quick slice.

13

What it won't do yet

Worth knowing before someone asks in a demo.

  • Emails and PDFs need credits on the account. Printing to Word and everything else works without them.
  • Deposits and down payments are not built. Invoice the order in full, or invoice what has been delivered.
  • Prices are typed, not priced by rule. There are no price lists yet, so a customer's special pricing is applied by hand.
  • Stock is not tracked. Delivered quantities are recorded on the order, not drawn from a warehouse.
  • Taxes on an invoice line come from the product, not from the order line, so check them if an order used unusual taxes.
  • No product picker grid. Add lines one at a time.
