- **19.0 was last changed on 23 Sep 2026.** Everything built in EMS since then (Inventory, Point of Sale,
  MyInvois, Activities, the dashboards, most of CRM) is not in it.
- **Documents:** worksheets 01–23 have hand-offs, but the status table in `REVIEWING.md` stops at 15; 16–23
  (Orders and the Sales work) have no row.
- **Ledger:** `build/ids.json` holds 18 worksheets, 60 workflows, 31 buttons, 35 views, 276 controls, 50 rules and
  770 records: Phase 1 and Sales.
- **Reference:** casimir.odoo.com is gone (its trial expired on 22 Sep, `CLAUDE.md`). What remains is the
  extracts in `reference/odoo-19.4/` and the 19.0 source, which is behind saas~19.4 in places. `DECISIONS.md`
  (15 Sep) still names the live tenant as the reference.
- **App name:** the builder scripts check the app's name against `('CRM odoo', 'ERP Master')` in `build/hap.py`.
  The app is now called EMS, so those checks stop every script.
- **Platform traps** in `BUILDING.md` were verified on hap-cli 0.8.31 (Sep). The CLI is now 0.9.14; traps found
  since are in `TRAPS-HAP-CLI-0.9.md`.
- **The repo is public** (GitHub answers anonymous requests). It carries the app id and the organisation id.
  Agreed on 25 Nov: the repo owner makes it private and adds the builders as collaborators.
